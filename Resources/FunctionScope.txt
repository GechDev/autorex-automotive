# SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

This Software Development Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **[Client Name]**, hereinafter referred to as the **"Client,"** and **[Your Company Name / Service Provider Name]**, hereinafter referred to as the **"Service Provider,"** collectively referred to as the **"Parties,"** on **[Effective Date]**.

The Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. SCOPE OF WORK

### 1.1 Project Overview

The Service Provider agrees to design, develop, migrate, and deploy a modern web-based software solution for **AutoRex Automotive** (the "Application").

The Application will provide a professional public-facing automotive website together with secure administrative and employee functionality intended to support the Client's day-to-day business operations.

The Application will replace and modernize the existing garage web application while preserving the established visual identity and improving its technical architecture, security, responsiveness, maintainability, and functionality.

The project will include:

* Public-facing AutoRex Automotive website
* Responsive web design
* Employee authentication and authorization
* Employee/user management
* Customer management
* Vehicle/customer information where applicable
* Service management
* Appointment management
* Administrative dashboard
* Secure private administrative pages
* Database-backed business operations
* Deployment and production configuration

---

### 1.2 Public-Facing Website

The Service Provider shall develop the following public-facing pages:

* Home
* About
* Services
* Individual Service Details
* Contact
* Appointment/Booking

The public website shall be responsive and optimized for:

* Desktop
* Laptop
* Tablet
* Mobile devices

The website shall maintain a consistent AutoRex Automotive visual language across all pages.

The existing AutoRex Home page and approved UI designs shall serve as the primary visual references for the implementation.

The Service Provider shall not substantially redesign approved UI designs without the Client's approval.

---

### 1.3 Authentication and User Management

The Application shall include secure authentication and authorization functionality.

This may include:

* Employee registration where applicable
* Employee login
* Logout
* Session management
* Password hashing
* Password validation
* Password reset functionality
* Email verification where configured
* Protected routes
* Role-based authorization
* Secure session cookies

The system shall support the following administrative roles:

* **Administrator**
* **Manager**
* **Employee**

The permissions associated with each role shall be implemented according to the agreed application requirements.

Public users shall not be permitted to create administrator accounts.

---

### 1.4 Customer Management

Authorized employees shall be able to manage customer records.

Customer management shall include, where applicable:

* Create customer
* View customer
* Update customer
* Search customers
* Delete or deactivate customer records where appropriate
* View customer-related appointments
* View relevant vehicle/service information

Access to customer information shall be restricted according to the user's authorization level.

---

### 1.5 Service Management

Authorized users shall be able to manage the automotive services displayed and/or managed by the Application.

This may include:

* Create services
* Edit services
* Update service descriptions
* Update service pricing where applicable
* Activate/deactivate services
* Display services publicly
* Manage service details

Public service information shall be presented consistently with the approved AutoRex Automotive design.

---

### 1.6 Appointment Management

The Application shall provide an appointment/booking workflow.

The appointment system shall allow customers to submit appointment requests containing appropriate information such as:

* Customer information
* Contact information
* Vehicle information where applicable
* Requested service
* Preferred date
* Preferred time
* Additional notes

Authorized employees shall be able to:

* View appointments
* Review appointment details
* Confirm appointments
* Update appointment status
* Cancel appointments
* Mark appointments as in progress
* Mark appointments as completed

The Application shall support the following appointment statuses:

* Pending
* Confirmed
* In Progress
* Completed
* Cancelled

---

### 1.7 Administrative Dashboard

The Application shall include a secure administrative dashboard for authorized users.

The dashboard may include:

* Overview/dashboard
* Appointment management
* Customer management
* Service management
* Employee management
* Relevant operational information
* Authentication and authorization controls

The dashboard shall be accessible only to authenticated and appropriately authorized users.

---

### 1.8 Private Pages

Private application pages shall require authentication.

Unauthenticated users attempting to access protected areas shall be redirected to the appropriate authentication page or shown an appropriate unauthorized response.

Authorization shall be enforced server-side and shall not rely solely on hiding interface elements.

---
